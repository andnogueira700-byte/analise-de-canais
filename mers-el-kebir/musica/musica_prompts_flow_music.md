# Música — prompts para o Flow Music (Mers-el-Kébir)

**Regras comuns a todos os prompts** (já embutidas nos textos): instrumental, sem vocal com letra, sem melodia "de herói", sem bateria eletrônica moderna, produção de trilha de documentário de guerra, mixagem que deixa espaço para uma voz masculina grave narrando por cima (frequências médias 1–3 kHz mais limpas, sem solos agudos o tempo todo). Gere cada faixa com 20–30 segundos a mais do que o trecho, para eu poder fazer fusões.

**Como usar:** cole o bloco "PROMPT" inteiro. Se o Flow Music aceitar campos separados, use "Estilo" (linha *Style*) e "Instruções" (o resto). Se alguma faixa sair muito diferente do pedido, me diga o número da faixa e o que não agradou.

## M1 — ABERTURA (tensão forte) · usar de 0:00 a 0:45 · gerar ~1:10

**PROMPT**
> Dark cinematic documentary opening, instrumental, 65 BPM, key of D minor. Starts with a single sustained low drone (sub bass and bowed contrabass) and a very quiet mechanical clock ticking in the center. At 0:08 a slow heartbeat-like pulse (low frame drum, felt-muffled) enters, one hit per beat. At 0:15 tremolo violins and violas begin, high and thin, creating anxiety, no melody. At 0:25 the pulse doubles in intensity with a distant metallic scrape (hull of a ship, steel on steel) and low brass swells, rising continuously. At 0:40 build to a sharp stop on a deep boom followed by 2 seconds of decaying reverb tail. Mood: ominous, suspense, historical tragedy about to happen. No melody, no drums kit, no electronic synths, no choir with words. War-documentary production, wide cinematic stereo, dark and dry, leave midrange free for a male narrator.

**Estilo (campo curto):** dark ambient cinematic suspense, orchestral, drone, ticking clock, war documentary

## M4 — O RELÓGIO DE MERS-EL-KÉBIR (suspense em 4 estágios) · usar de 5:27 a 9:28 · gerar ~4:30

**PROMPT**
> Long suspense piece for a naval standoff, instrumental, starts at 60 BPM and gradually accelerates to 84 BPM over 4 minutes, key of F# minor. Structure in four stages, with seamless transitions. STAGE 1 (0:00–0:50): quiet atmosphere with a low drone, sea-like noise textures, one lonely pizzicato cello note every few seconds, calm but uneasy. STAGE 2 (0:50–1:50): a clock-like pulse on muted woodblock and low piano, tremolo violas appear, uneasy tension grows, a faint brass chord swell every 8 bars. STAGE 3 (1:50–3:10): rhythmic string ostinato (staccato, 16th notes, repeating pattern) with low timpani rolls, tension clearly building, short brass stabs. STAGE 4 (3:10–4:30): high sustained violins with trills, quickening heartbeat percussion, brass chords rising chromatically, ends abruptly on a suspended chord with no resolution and 1 second of silence at the very end. Mood: waiting for a deadline that cannot be undone, every minute worse than the last. Not heroic, not melodic; dry, cinematic, documentary war suspense; leave midrange free for narration.

**Estilo:** cinematic suspense, ticking clock, string ostinato, rising tension, war documentary
